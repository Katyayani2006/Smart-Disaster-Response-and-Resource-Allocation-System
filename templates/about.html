About Smart Disaster Response

Rapidly predict incident severity, prioritize emergency response, and allocate relief resources.

How it works

Use the Predict page to submit disaster details. The app then analyzes severity, infrastructure damage, accessibility, and historical occurrences to recommend a response priority.

Higher priority events receive more rescue teams, food, medical support, and supplies. The Dashboard gives a quick view of incident distribution and resource planning.

Purpose

This system is designed to support emergency planning teams, disaster response coordinators, and local management authorities with fast, actionable insights.
